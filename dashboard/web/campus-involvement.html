Methodology

Public RSS until API access lands

Events come from the public CampusGroups RSS feed. The heatmap uses a fixed academic-year window (Monday on or before August 24 through Friday on or after May 21; summer omitted). The server refreshes the feed every few minutes. Private locations stay masked as published. Multi-day events count on every day they span.
