Methodology

Final IPEDS data, clearly separated by year and cohort

Final NCES data. The median is the middle institution after sorting; the mean is the arithmetic average. Means move more when a few institutions have unusually high or low values, so medians remain the default comparison. Ranges show the middle half of each group. Marist is excluded from both groups.

Collection year labels the IPEDS release. Measurement years and graduation cohorts may differ and appear with each measure. Missing or suppressed values remain unavailable, never zero.
